- **The ledger can live in your Obsidian vault (#41).** `obsidian_link` surfaces a project's ledger inside a vault: `link` (default) symlinks it into `<vault>/break-free/<project>` so a note edited in Obsidian is the same bytes git sees, `index` writes a single note pointing at it, and `off` does nothing. Detection reads Obsidian's own vault registry rather than scanning the filesystem, so it is exact and cannot wander; a machine without Obsidian stays silent rather than warning about not having it. It never copies, and it refuses rather than overwrites — anything at the target that is not our own link to that same ledger is left untouched, because a vault is someone's notes. `--doctor` now reports the detected vault and the configured graph provider.
